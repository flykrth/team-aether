## 45-Second Pitch Script

### [0:00 - 0:12] The Hook & The Problem
> *"Did you know that in modern healthcare, dental clinics operate completely blind to hospital records? When a patient sits in the dental chair for an extraction, the dentist has no real-time way of knowing if they're taking high-potency blood thinners or have a prosthetic heart valve. One forgotten question on a paper clipboard can cause fatal surgical hemorrhage or life-threatening heart infections."*

### [0:13 - 0:28] The Solution: MDIN
> *"Meet **MDIN**: The Medical-Dental Interoperability Node for CareStack. MDIN bridges CareStack with enterprise medical EHRs using HL7 FHIR R4 and sub-second CDS Hooks. The moment a dentist selects a surgical extraction or scaling procedure, MDIN translates medical diagnoses, labs, and medications into real-time chairside safety guidance."*

### [0:29 - 0:45] The Impact & Callout
> *"From preventing Warfarin hemorrhages to enforcing AHA antibiotic prophylaxis and safeguarding diabetic healing, MDIN brings true medical-dental interoperability to the dental chair. Built by Team Aether for DSOLVE 2026. Tagging @Drishti and @CareStack!"*
